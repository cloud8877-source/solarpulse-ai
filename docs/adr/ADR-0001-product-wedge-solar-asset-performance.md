# ADR-0001 — Product Wedge: Solar Asset Performance Copilot

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-06-22

## Context

The team is registered for MAIC T1 Clean Energy. The product must be technically credible, commercially viable, track-relevant, scalable, and aligned with Malaysia's clean-energy priorities.

Several product wedges were considered: grid demand forecasting, project development intelligence, BESS/EV optimization, ESG reporting, and generic clean-energy chat.

## Decision

Build **SolarPulse AI**, a solar asset performance and O&M intelligence copilot.

The product focuses on:
- solar generation forecasting,
- expected-vs-actual performance,
- anomaly detection,
- likely cause explanation,
- action recommendation,
- kWh/RM/CO2 impact reporting.

## Rationale

This wedge best balances:
- T1 track fit,
- public/open data availability,
- demo feasibility,
- commercial buyer clarity,
- ability to show load-bearing AI,
- expansion path into project development, BESS, EV, ESG, and demand response.

## Consequences

- The MVP will focus on PV asset intelligence, not generic ESG or grid control.
- Demo fixtures are acceptable if clearly labeled.
- Future modules can extend into project-development intelligence and BESS/EV optimization.
